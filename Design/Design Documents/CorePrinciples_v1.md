# A Dog's Life

## Core Design Principles v1.0

---

## Core Philosophy

**Complexity does not replace hope. Complexity deepens hope.**

---

## Protagonist Principle

**The dog does not change. His understanding grows.**

---

## Player Perspective

**The player experiences the world through the dog's senses, instincts and behaviours.**

---

## Expression Principle

**The dog's actions communicate his emotions before his words do.**

---

## World Principle

**The world exists beyond the dog; every creature has its own story, motivations and history.**

---

## Storytelling Principle

**The player should feel understanding through observation and experience, not receive it through exposition.**

---

## Design Principle

**Every decision should strengthen the dog's perspective, reinforce the themes, and preserve hope.**
