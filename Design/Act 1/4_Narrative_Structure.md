# Act I – Forest

## Layer 4 — Narrative Structure

---

## Purpose

Layer 4 defines the narrative structure of the act.

It establishes the key story beats that carry the emotional journey from beginning to end, ensuring each event serves the purpose established in the previous layers.

---

## Inciting Incident

The act begins with the dog waking from a dream of the previous day's walk.

He stretches and yawns before suddenly stopping, as though hearing an unfamiliar sound.

He sniffs once.

Twice.

Three times.

In the air.

At the ground.

All around.

> "Where are you?"

Finding no trace of his owner, his head and ears lower for a brief moment.

For the first time in his life, he experiences doubt.

Then his ears rise once more.

He looks ahead.

> "You'll return. You always return."

Rain begins to fall.

A distant howl echoes through the forest.

Or perhaps it is thunder.

> "I'll find you."

The inciting incident is not the dog's awakening, but his response to it.

Choosing hope over uncertainty, he resolves to keep searching.

From this moment, the story shifts from **a lost dog** to **a dog on a journey home**.

## External Direction of the Narrative

Following the loss of the scent, the dog continues searching alone until he encounters the first meaningful character of the story.

The purpose of this encounter is not simply to provide direction, but to introduce the dog to the reality that other creatures have desires and motivations of their own.

The first NPC earns the dog's trust through sincerity and kindness, becoming the first character to influence the direction of his journey. In doing so, they also pursue a goal of their own.

Although the dog ultimately receives the help he needs, he later realises that the encounter was not entirely selfless.

This moment is not intended as a betrayal, nor is it meant to diminish the dog's optimism or faith in others.

Instead, it marks a subtle refinement of his character.

The dog begins the story with **unconditional, instinctive trust**.

He leaves the encounter with **conditional, instinctive trust**.

His nature remains unchanged. He is still hopeful, loving and trusting.

However, he now understands that others do not always want the same things he does, and that kindness and self-interest are not mutually exclusive.

The lesson is gentle rather than painful.

The dog is not hardened by the experience.

He is simply a little wiser than before.
